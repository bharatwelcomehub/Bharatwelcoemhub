1. Getting Started

Visit Our Website

Access Purnabramha at: www.purnabramha.com or app.purnabramha.com

Create an Account (Optional)

1
Click "Login / Sign Up" button (top right corner)
2
Choose: Sign up with Email or Continue with Google
3
Fill in your details: Name, Email, Phone, Password
4
Click "Create Account" - You're ready!
You can browse the menu and place orders without creating an account too!

2. How to Order Pickup 🛍️

Order your favorite Maharashtrian food and pick it up from your nearest Purnabramha center.

Step-by-Step Guide:

1
Go to "Pickup Order" from the menu
2
Select your Region: India or Australia
3
Choose your nearest center (HSR Bangalore, Pune, Perth, etc.)
4
Select Pickup Date and Time
5
Browse the menu and Add items to your cart
6
Review your order and click "Order via WhatsApp"
7
Confirm on WhatsApp - Your order is placed!
⏰ Minimum Order: ₹200 (India) | $20 (Australia)
📍 Pickup Time: Select your preferred slot

3. Book a Table 🪑

Reserve a table for dine-in at any Purnabramha location.

Booking Steps:

1
Click "Table Booking" from menu
2
Select Region and Center
3
Choose Date (up to 30 days in advance)
4
Select Time Slot (12 PM - 10 PM)
5
Enter Number of Guests
6
Add special occasion (Birthday, Anniversary, etc.) if any
7
Fill contact details and Confirm Booking
Book at least 2 hours in advance for same-day reservations.
