Login & Authentication

intra.purnabramha.com/login
1

Open the Portal

Visit intra.purnabramha.com in your browser.

2

Select Your Center

From the dropdown, select your assigned center (e.g., PB-TH for Thane, PB-HSR for HSR Layout).

3

Enter Mobile Number

Enter your registered mobile number.

4

Click "Send OTP"

An OTP will be sent to your registered email.

5

Enter OTP & Verify

Enter the 6-digit OTP from your email and click Verify.

ImportantYou can only login with the center assigned to you. If you need access to a different center, contact your Super Admin.
2

Your Dashboard

After Login

After login, your sidebar shows only the modules assigned to you:

  • Attendance — Mark daily attendance for your center's employees
  • Sales & Cash — Enter daily sales, expenses, and view reports
  • HR Management — View employees, salary details, and generate payslips
NoteYou will only see data for your assigned center. To view other centers or access Accounts/Franchise modules, additional permissions are needed from Super Admin.
3

Attendance Management

Attendance > India Centers
1

Open Attendance

Click Attendance > India Centers in the sidebar. Your center is pre-selected.

2

Select Month

Use the month picker to select the current month (default) or view previous months.

3

Mark Attendance

You see a calendar grid with all employees listed on the left. Click on any cell to mark:

  • P = Present (Full day)
  • A = Absent
  • H = Half Day
  • WO = Week Off
  • L = Leave
4

Auto-Save

Each click auto-saves the attendance status. No separate save button needed.

Daily TaskMark attendance for today before end of day. Previous day's attendance gets auto-frozen at midnight and cannot be edited without Super Admin intervention.
4

Daily Sales Entry

Sales & Cash > Sales Dashboard > Sales Entry
1

Open Sales Dashboard

Click Sales & Cash > Sales Dashboard.

2

Go to Sales Entry Tab

Click the "Sales Entry" tab.

3

Select Today's Date

The date defaults to today. Ensure it's correct before entering data.

4

Enter Sales Data

Fill in all sales fields:

  • Cash Sales — Total cash collected
  • Card Sales — POS card transactions
  • Swiggy — Swiggy online orders
  • Zomato — Zomato online orders
  • Other Online — Any other platforms
  • Total Guests — Number of guests served
  • Total Bills — Number of bills generated
5

Save

Click "Save". The Overview tab will update with the latest figures.

Alternative: Grid UpdateFor faster entry across multiple dates, use the "Grid Update" tab which shows a spreadsheet-like view of the entire month.
5

Daily Expense Entry

Sales & Cash > Sales Dashboard > Expense Entry
1

Open Expense Entry

Go to Sales Dashboard and click the "Expense Entry" tab.

2

Select Date

Default is today. You can switch to Range mode to view all expenses in a period.

3

Add New Expense

Fill in the fields at the top:

  • Description — What was purchased (e.g., "Vegetables from vendor")
  • Amount — Total cost
  • Category — Select from dropdown (GROCERY, DAIRY PRODUCTS, PAV, CYLINDER, etc.)
  • Payment Mode — CASH, UPI, CARD, BANK TRANSFER, etc.

Click "Add Expense".

4

Batch Entry (Multiple Items)

Click "Add 3 More Entries" to add multiple rows. Fill all rows and click "Save All".

5

Edit Existing Expenses

All rows in the table are editable. Change any field (description, amount, category, mode) and click "Save All".

6

Attach Bills

Click the paperclip icon on any expense to upload a bill photo or PDF. Supported: JPG, PNG, WEBP (5MB), PDF (10MB).

7

Group Under Invoice

Select multiple expenses using checkboxes, then click "Group Selected" to link them under one common invoice.

Frozen DatesPrevious day's expenses are automatically frozen at midnight. You cannot edit frozen data. If corrections are needed, contact Super Admin to unfreeze.
6

HR Management > Employees

View Employees

Salary & Payslips

HR Management > Salary / Payslips
1

View Salary Summary

Go to HR Management > Salary. Select your center and month to see salary calculations.

2

Generate Payslip

Go to HR Management > Payslips. Select:

  • Center — Your center (pre-selected)
  • Month — Salary month

Click "Generate PDF" to download the payslip.

8

Freeze & Data Lock

Understanding Data Protection

The system automatically protects finalized data:

  • Auto Freeze — Previous day's sales and expenses are locked at midnight
  • Admin Freeze — Super Admin can manually lock any date
  • Frozen data appears with a lock icon and cannot be edited
  • To correct frozen data — Contact your Super Admin to temporarily unfreeze the date
Best PracticeEnter all sales and expenses on the same day to avoid data lock issues. Set a daily routine: End of day = Enter data.
