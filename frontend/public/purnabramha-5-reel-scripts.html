🎬 Production Tips for All Reels

📱 Format & Technical

  • Shoot in 9:16 vertical format (1080x1920)
  • Use good lighting - natural light preferred for food shots
  • Add captions/subtitles for accessibility
  • Keep text within safe zones (not too close to edges)

🍽️ Food Shots Tips

  • Show steam rising from hot dishes
  • Use slow motion for pouring/serving
  • Close-up texture shots of Poha, Misal, etc.
  • Show the "cheese pull" equivalent - stretchy Shrikhand, flowing Aamras

🎵 Music & Audio

  • Use trending audio when possible for algorithm boost
  • Ensure voiceover is clear and audible over music
  • Add sound effects: sizzling, crunching, pouring
  • Keep music upbeat and energetic

📊 Posting Strategy

  • Best times: 7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM
  • Frequency: 4-5 reels per week
  • Engage: Reply to comments within first hour
  • Cross-post: Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook simultaneously
