Attendance Module

Menu → Attendance → India Centers / International
▼

📚 What This Screen Does

The Attendance module is a monthly grid where you mark the attendance status of every employee at each center. It tracks IN (Present), OFF (Day Off), HALF (Half Day), and ABSENT for every day of the month.

✅ What You Can Do Here

  • Select any center and month to view/edit attendance
  • Mark attendance: IN, OFF, HALF, ABSENT for each employee each day
  • View transferred employees with their historical data preserved
  • Bulk update attendance for multiple employees
  • Lock/Unlock months to prevent accidental changes
  • View the Attendance Dashboard for center-wide summary analytics

🎯 Why This Matters

Attendance directly feeds into salary calculation. Any error here will result in wrong salary payouts. This is the foundation of payroll accuracy.

Common Mistakes to AvoidDo not forget to mark attendance for transferred employees. Their data stays in the original center for the days they worked there.
Do NotDo not edit a locked month without approval. Locking prevents accidental changes after salary is processed.

👤 Who Can Access

  • Super Admin: All centers
  • Admin: Configured centers
  • Center Manager: Own center only

Sales & Cash Summary

Menu → Sales & Cash → Sales Dashboard
▼

📚 What This Screen Does

The Sales Dashboard shows daily sales data broken down by payment mode: Cash, Card, UPI (BharatPay), Swiggy, Zomato, and other online channels. It includes a Grid Bulk Update feature for fast data entry.

✅ What You Can Do Here

  • View monthly sales summary for any center
  • Enter daily sales via the Grid Bulk Update (row per day, column per payment mode)
  • View total sales, online vs cash split, aggregator breakdowns
  • Compare month-on-month sales trends
  • Track GST-inclusive and exclusive amounts

🎯 Why This Matters

Sales data is the primary input for PIB, MIS, Revenue Share calculation, and MG (Minimum Guarantee) Payout. Incorrect sales entry leads to wrong franchise payouts and financial statements.

Example UsageAt the end of each day, the center manager enters the total sales breakdown. At month-end, the accountant verifies and locks the data for PIB generation.
Do NotDo not enter aggregator sales (Swiggy/Zomato) amounts that include GST. Enter the net amount received from the aggregator platform.

📊 Reports Available

  • Daily Sales Export (Excel)

Commission Tracking

Menu → Accounts → Center Accounts → Commission Tab
▼

📚 What This Screen Does

Upload monthly commission Excel reports from aggregator platforms and bank statements. The system parses the data and calculates exact deductions, MDR charges, and sundry debtors.

✅ What You Can Do Here

  • Swiggy/Zomato/DoorDash: Single file upload. System parses Gross Amount, GST on Commission, TDS, Other Deductions, and Net Payout
  • Cards: Dual file upload (EDC Statement + Bank Statement). System matches transactions and calculates MDR Bank Charges
  • PhonePe: Dual file upload (EDC + Bank Statement). System calculates Sundry Debtors (next-day settlement amounts)
  • View parsed results with 4-card breakdown before confirming
  • Re-upload to replace previous data for the same month/platform

🎯 Why This Matters

Commissions are one of the largest deductions from franchise revenue. Accurate commission tracking ensures the franchise is not over-paying or under-reporting deductions. The dual-file matching for Cards/PhonePe provides bank-reconciled accuracy.

ExampleUpload the Swiggy monthly statement Excel → System shows: Gross = Rs. 1,50,000, GST = Rs. 2,700, TDS = Rs. 150, Net = Rs. 1,47,150 → Confirm to save.
ImportantFor Cards and PhonePe, you MUST upload BOTH files (EDC + Bank Statement). The system needs both to calculate accurate MDR/Sundry amounts.

PIB Report (Profit & Investment Brief)

Menu → Accounts → Center Accounts → Generate PIB
▼

📚 What This Screen Does

The PIB is the official monthly financial report for each franchise center. It shows the complete profit calculation from Total Sales down to the final Revenue Share / MG amount payable.

📄 PIB Financial Summary Structure

  • Total Sales
  • Less: Total Expenses
  • Less: Total Commissions (uploaded)
  • Less: GST on Sales (5% for India, 10% for Australia)
  • = NET REVENUE
  • Revenue Share % applied on Net Revenue
  • Compared with Minimum Guarantee (MG)
  • Payable = Higher of Revenue Share or MG

🎯 Why This Matters

The PIB is the single document that determines what the franchise pays Purnabramha each month. It is the most critical financial document in the system.

DownloadsPIB PDF Report with Purnabramha branding, Financial Summary table, Revenue Share calculation, and Working Capital details.

Monthly Finance Workflow

Steps to complete each month's financial closing
▼

📋 Step-by-Step Monthly Process

  • Step 1: Verify all daily sales entries are complete for the month (Sales Dashboard)
  • Step 2: Verify all expense entries are complete with invoices attached
  • Step 3: Upload commission Excel files for each platform:
    • Swiggy statement → Single file
    • Zomato statement → Single file
    • Cards → EDC file + Bank Statement (dual upload)
    • PhonePe → EDC file + Bank Statement (dual upload)
  • Step 4: Verify the 4-card commission breakdown: Gross → GST/Tax → Other Deductions → Net Payout
  • Step 5: Generate PIB Report and verify Financial Summary
  • Step 6: Check MIS Dashboard for overall business health
  • Step 7: Record franchise payments in MG & Payout section
  • Step 8: Export MG Payout as Excel/PDF for records
ImportantAlways upload commissions BEFORE generating PIB. The PIB uses commission data to calculate Net Revenue. Without commissions, the Net Revenue and Revenue Share will be overstated.
Pro TipUse the month range filter in MG & Payout to view the full payout history. Export as PDF for franchise owner meetings.
Key Reports You Generate
  • PIB Report (PDF): Monthly profit statement per center
  • GST Report (PDF): GST breakdown for tax filing
  • Commission Summary (PDF): Platform-wise commission details
  • MG Payout Export (Excel/PDF): Month-wise payment tracking
  • Invoice ZIP: All expense invoices in a downloadable ZIP
