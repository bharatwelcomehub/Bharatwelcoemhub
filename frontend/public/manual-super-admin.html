Super Admin Access As Super Admin, you have unrestricted access to every module, every center, and every configuration setting. You can manage users, assign roles, sync master data, freeze/unfreeze dates, and oversee all financial data across all branches.

Login & Authentication

intra.purnabramha.com/login
1

Select Your Center

Open the portal. From the dropdown, select PB-MGT (Management center).

2

Enter Mobile Number

Enter your registered Super Admin mobile number (e.g., 97413XXXXX or 99608XXXXX).

3

Click "Send OTP"

An OTP will be sent to your registered email address.

4

Enter OTP & Verify

Enter the 6-digit OTP and click Verify. You will be redirected to the dashboard.

TipOTP is sent to the email linked with your mobile number in the system. Check your inbox if you don't see it immediately.

3

Center Management

Management > Centers
1

View All Centers

Navigate to Management > Centers. See a list of all active centers with their codes, names, and regions (India / International).

2

Add New Center

Click "Add Center". Fill in the center code (e.g., PB-NEW), name, address, phone, and region. Click Save.

3

Edit Center Details

Click the pencil icon on any center row to update its information.

ImportantCenter codes must be unique and follow the PB-XXX format. Once created, center codes cannot be changed as they are referenced across all modules.
4

Manager Management

Management > Managers
1

View All Managers

See a list of all registered managers with their center assignments and roles.

2

Add New Manager

Click "Add Manager". Provide: Name, Mobile Number, Email, Assigned Center, and Role Key (e.g., center_manager, accountant).

3

Assign Roles

Each manager can be assigned specific role permissions (attendance, sales_cash, hr, accounting, franchise, operations, billing). Use Role Management for fine-grained control.

TipA manager's mobile number is their login credential. Make sure it's correct to allow them to receive OTPs.
5

Role Management

Management > Role Management
1

Navigate to Role Management

Go to Management > Role Management. You'll see a list of all managers with their current role assignments.

2

Edit Role Permissions

Click on a manager to expand their role toggles. Enable/Disable access modules: Attendance, Sales & Cash, HR, Accounting, Franchise, Operations, Billing.

3

Save Changes

After toggling the required permissions, click Save. The manager's sidebar will update on their next login.

Role Keys center_manager = Day-to-day center operations (attendance, sales, HR)
accountant = Financial access (accounts, MIS, loans, sales view)
franchise_owner = View-only franchise dashboard
admin = Extended admin access
6

Master Data Sync

Management > Master Data
1

Open Master Data

Navigate to Management > Master Data. This is the single source of truth for centers, employees, and franchise data.

2

Upload Excel Sheet

Click "Upload Master Data". Select the master Excel file containing employee lists, center details, and franchise information.

3

Confirm Sync

Review the changes detected. Click Confirm Sync to update all collections. Existing data is preserved — only new/changed records are updated.

WarningMaster Data sync updates employees, centers, and franchise mappings across the entire system. Always verify the Excel file before uploading. Existing roles and special configurations are NOT overwritten.

Attendance Module

Attendance > India Centers / International
1

Select Center & Month

Choose a center from the dropdown and select the month/year.

2

View Attendance Grid

A calendar grid shows each employee's daily status: Present (P), Absent (A), Half-day (H), Week Off (WO), Leave (L).

Attendance Dashboard

Expense Management

Sales & Cash > Sales Dashboard > Expense Entry
1

Open Expense Entry

Go to Sales Dashboard and click the "Expense Entry" tab.

2

Select Date

Pick a single date or switch to Range mode to view expenses across a period.

3

Add New Expense

Fill in Description, Amount, select Category (Expense Type) and Payment Mode. Click "Add Expense".

4

Batch Entry

Click "Add 3 More Entries" to add multiple expenses at once. Fill all rows and click "Save All".

5

Edit Existing

All fields in the expense table are editable inline. Change any value and click "Save All" at the top.

6

Attach Bills & Invoices

Click the paperclip icon to upload a PDF/image bill. Group multiple expenses under one invoice using the link icon.

Expense HeadsManage expense categories from Sales & Cash > Expense Heads. Add, edit, or deactivate expense types.

Center Accounts

Accounts > Center Accounts
1

Select Center & Month

Choose a center and month, then click Refresh to load the financial summary.

2

Financial Summary Tab

View KPIs: Total Sales, Net Revenue, Commissions, GST, and the full financial breakdown.

3

Revenue/Profit Share Tab

View the step-by-step calculation:
Total Sales - 5% GST - Commissions = Net Revenue
Then see the Franchise Owner vs Purnabramha split (e.g., 15/85).
For India: 18% GST on revenue share is shown for reference but NOT added to Purnabramha's total.

4

Commission Upload

Upload monthly commission data (Swiggy, Zomato, Card charges) via the Commission tab.

5

Payout Summary & MG Payout

Track Minimum Guarantee payouts. Use FROM/TO month range, export as Excel or PDF.

6

Generate PDFs

Click "Generate PIB Report" or "Generate GST Summary" to create official financial documents.

Franchise Exit & Closure

Franchise > Exit & Closure
1

Initiate Exit

Click "Initiate Exit". Select the franchise and provide exit reason and effective date.

2

Signature Collection

The exit agreement requires signatures from: Franchisor Signatories (Sandeep/Jayanti), Exit Manager, and Franchisee Directors (auto-populated from franchise data).

3

Generate Agreement PDF

Once all parties have signed, generate the official exit agreement document.

ImportantExit agreement data is permanent. Once signatures are collected, the record should not be modified. Use the migration endpoint only if upgrading from old signature format.

Freeze Control

Sales & Cash > Sales Dashboard > Freeze Control
1

Open Freeze Control

Go to Sales Dashboard and click the "Freeze Control" tab.

2

Auto Freeze

Previous day's data is automatically frozen at midnight. Center managers cannot edit frozen dates.

3

Admin Freeze/Unfreeze

As Super Admin, you can manually freeze or unfreeze any date for any center. This overrides auto-freeze.

Best PracticeFreeze the month-end data after all entries are verified. This prevents accidental modifications to finalized data.
