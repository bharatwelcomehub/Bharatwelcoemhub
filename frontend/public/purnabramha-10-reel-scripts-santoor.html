🎬 Production Tips for All 10 Reels

🎵 Music Selection

Search for: "Santoor instrumental royalty free" | "Indian classical fusion" | "Pandit Shivkumar Sharma style" on YouTube Audio Library, Epidemic Sound, or Artlist. Ensure no vocals - only instruments.

📱 Video Format

9:16 vertical | 1080x1920 resolution | 30-45 seconds | Add captions for accessibility

🍽️ Food Shots Must-Haves

Steam rising from hot dishes | Slow motion pouring/drizzling | Close-up textures | Golden hour lighting | Clean backgrounds

🎤 Voice Over Tips

Warm, inviting tone | Natural Hindi-English mix | Pause at emotional moments | Match tempo with music | Professional recording (no echo)

⏰ Best Posting Times

India: 7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM | Australia: 11 AM - 1 PM, 5-7 PM | Frequency: 4-5 reels per week
