3

Center Accounts

Accounts > Center Accounts
1

Select Center & Month

Choose a center from the dropdown and pick the month. Click Refresh.

2

View Financial Summary

The Financial Summary tab shows KPIs:

  • Total Sales (all channels combined)
  • Net Revenue (after GST and commissions)
  • Total Commissions (Swiggy, Zomato, Card charges)
  • Total Expenses
  • GST calculations
3

Navigate Tabs

Use the tabs at the top: Financial Summary, Revenue/Profit Share, Commission, Payout Summary, MG Payout.

4

Revenue / Profit Share

Center Accounts > Revenue/Profit Share Tab

This tab shows the complete financial calculation breakdown — step by step:

India Revenue Calculation (Example)

Total SalesRs. 7,83,480
Less: GST on Sales (5%)- Rs. 39,174
Less: Total Commissions- Rs. 25,000
= Net Revenue (Base for Split)Rs. 7,19,306

Revenue Share Split (15/85)

Franchise Owner (15%)Rs. 1,07,896
Purnabramha (85%)Rs. 6,11,410
Revenue Share GST (18%) — for reference onlyRs. 1,10,054
Purnabramha Total PayableRs. 6,11,410
Key Points India: 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9%) on Revenue Share is shown for reference but NOT added to Purnabramha's total payable.
Australia: 10% GST is applied on Profit Share and IS added to total payable.
5

Commission Upload

Center Accounts > Commission Tab
1

Open Commission Tab

In Center Accounts, click the Commission tab after selecting center and month.

2

Upload Commission Files

Upload the monthly commission statements from platforms:

  • Swiggy Commission — Upload Swiggy settlement report
  • Zomato Commission — Upload Zomato settlement report
  • Card/Bank Charges — Upload POS transaction charges
  • Other Deductions — Any additional platform deductions
3

Verify Totals

After upload, verify the commission totals match the platform statements. The system auto-calculates GST on commissions and other deductions.

ImportantCommission data directly affects the Revenue Share calculation and MIS Dashboard figures. Double-check before uploading.

9

Sales Data (Read Access)

Sales & Cash > Sales Dashboard

As an Accountant, you have read-only access to Sales & Cash for all centers:

  • View daily sales figures across all centers
  • View expense entries and summaries
  • Download Excel reports
  • View payment breakdowns
NoteYou can view but not edit sales and expense data unless you have additional permissions. For corrections, coordinate with the Center Manager or Super Admin.
10

PDF Reports

Center Accounts

Generate official financial documents from Center Accounts:

  • PIB Report — Profit & Income Before tax report with full financial breakdown including 5% GST deduction display
  • GST Summary — Monthly GST calculation summary for filing
  • Revenue Share Invoice — Franchise owner's share calculation
  • MG Payout Report — Minimum Guarantee payout history (Excel/PDF)
TipGenerate all reports for a month AFTER commission data is uploaded and verified. This ensures accurate figures in all documents.
