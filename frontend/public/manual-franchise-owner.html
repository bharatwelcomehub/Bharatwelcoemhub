Open the Portal

Financial Insights NEW

Owner Dashboard > Financial Insights Tab

Your center's executive-level financial analytics in one screen. Same data is also available inside Owner Reports as a collapsible section, so you can use whichever feels easier.

What's inside? AI Executive Summary — a 1-paragraph narrative auto-written by GPT-5.2 explaining the period in plain English.
6 KPI cards — Sales, Expenses, Commission, GST, Gross Profit, Net P/L — with % of sales and trend arrows.
Sales Channels — Dine-in vs Swiggy / Zomato / DoorDash split with dependency %.
Financial Ratios — Food Cost, Salary, Rent, Utility, Commission Burden, Aggregator Share — each marked Good / Warning / Critical.
12-Month Profit Trend — bar strip showing P/L trajectory (green = profit, red = loss).
Expense Segregation — top categories ranked by amount + % of sales.
Month-by-Month Breakdown — visible when you pick a range or FY.
Per-Center Comparison — visible when you select multiple centers.
1

Pick the Period

Choose from Single Month, Month Range, Financial Year (e.g., FY 2025-26), or Custom Date Range. The default is the current month.

2

Pick Center(s)

Centers appear as pill buttons. Click to toggle. As an Owner, you only see your franchise centers — selection is automatic if you have one.

3

Apply Filters

Click Apply Filters. Numbers refresh and the AI summary regenerates within ~5–10 seconds.

4

Download Reports

Click Excel or CSV to download the full analytics pack (5-sheet workbook: Summary, Ratios, 12-Month Trend, By Month, Expense Categories).

How to read the ratios Food Cost — ideal ≤ 32% of sales. Above means raw-material waste or pricing pressure.
Salary — ideal ≤ 22%. Above suggests overstaffing or weak sales.
Rent — ideal ≤ 10%. Above means rent is too high vs sales (or sales need scaling).
Commission Burden — ideal ≤ 6%. Aggregators eating too much margin.
Net Margin — healthy ≥ 10%, excellent ≥ 15%.
The numbers here = the numbers everywhereThis screen pulls from the same canonical financial engine that powers MIS, PIB and Center Accounts — so reconciliation never breaks. If a number looks off, contact the accounts team with the screenshot.

Center Profitability Health Dashboard NEW

Sidebar > Accounts > Center Health Dashboard

A purpose-built single-screen "truth view" of your center for any month. Numbers + AI commentary + downloadable PDF you can share inside the family / with directors.

What you'll see Health Score (0–100) — a single composite score with a tier label: Excellent / Stable / Warning / Critical / Dangerous.
Founder's Monthly Reality Check — short AI-written paragraph that calls out what's working, what's not, and what to focus on.
Profitability Formula card — Sales − Commission − GST = Net Revenue; − Expenses = Net P/L. Same math everywhere.
Working Capital Health — current WC vs base, % of base, status, with a coloured progress bar.
Operational Ratios — same 6 ratios as Financial Insights, with badges.
12-Month P/L Trend bar strip.
Leakage Analysis — flagged signals (overstaffing, aggregator dependency, food-cost leakage, sales drop, etc.) with severity + estimated monthly impact in ₹.
What Needs Correction — a numbered action list specific to your active leakages.
Predictive 3-Month Warning — AI projection of where things head if the current pattern continues.
1

Open the Dashboard

From the sidebar, click Accounts → Center Health Dashboard. (Available to Franchise Owners and Accounts.)

2

Pick Center + Month

Select your center and the month you want to review. Data + AI narrative load automatically.

3

Download PDF

Click Download PDF (top-right). You get a clean, share-ready report with score, narrative, leakages and a 12-month trend table. Forward it to your franchise directors or accountant.

Reading the score 85–100 Excellent — center is firing on all cylinders.
70–84 Stable — healthy, minor optimisations possible.
50–69 Warning — at least one important signal is off. Review the leakages.
30–49 Critical — multiple leakages active. Take corrective action this month.
0–29 Dangerous — escalate immediately. Profitability or working capital is at risk.
AI narratives are advisoryThe Founder's Reality Check and the 3-Month Warning are AI-generated to help you read the numbers faster — they are not financial commitments or audit opinions. The numbers underneath, however, are canonical.

Revenue Share Breakdown

Owner Dashboard > Revenue Share

This is the most important section — it shows exactly how your share is calculated, step by step:

How Your Revenue Share is Calculated

Total Sales (all channels)Rs. X,XX,XXX
Less: GST on Sales (5%)- Rs. XX,XXX
Less: Commissions (Swiggy, Zomato, Cards)- Rs. XX,XXX
= Net RevenueRs. X,XX,XXX

Revenue Split

Your Share (Franchise Owner) — 15%Rs. X,XX,XXX
Purnabramha (Franchisor) — 85%Rs. X,XX,XXX
Understanding the Numbers Total Sales = Everything your center earned from all sources
5% GST = Government tax deducted from sales (India)
Commissions = Fees charged by Swiggy, Zomato, card processing
Net Revenue = What remains after above deductions
Your Share = Your percentage of the Net Revenue (per your franchise agreement)
GST on Revenue Share For India: 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9%) is applicable on revenue share. This is shown for reference but not added to Purnabramha's total payable amount.
Your revenue share percentage is defined in your franchise agreement (typically 15%).
Questions about your share?If any number doesn't look right, contact the Purnabramha accounts team with the specific month and center. They can provide detailed commission statements and expense breakdowns.
